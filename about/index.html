Behind the curtain

About Us

FrankiesFunHouse.org is an independent, fan-made guide and future browser portal for Frankie's Fun House. Our goal is to give players a focused place to understand the point-and-click puzzle format, learn the basic controls, view representative screenshots, and eventually launch a browser-compatible version when one is properly available.

Our relationship to the game

This website is not operated by StudioBigBun and is not the official home of Frankie's Fun House. StudioBigBun created the game. Game names, characters, artwork, screenshots, and related intellectual property belong to their respective owners. We use a limited selection of public promotional images to identify and discuss the game.

What we publish

We write original explanatory content for players looking for a free online entry point, gameplay overview, control notes, puzzle advice, and technical guidance. We clearly label the current game frame as unavailable instead of presenting an empty or unrelated embed as a working game. Official downloads, creator announcements, patch notes, and definitive release information should be checked on the creator's itch.io page.
