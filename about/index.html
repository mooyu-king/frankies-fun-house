Behind the curtain

About Us

FrankiesFunHouse.org is an independent, fan-made browser portal and guide for Frankie's Fun House. Our goal is to give players a focused place to play the browser edition, understand the point-and-click puzzle format, learn the controls, and find original walkthrough help when a puzzle blocks progress.

Our relationship to the game

This website is not operated by StudioBigBun and is not the official home of Frankie's Fun House. StudioBigBun created the game. Game names, characters, artwork, screenshots, and related intellectual property belong to their respective owners. We use game imagery only to identify, explain, and document the title for players.

What we publish

We publish original gameplay explanations, control notes, screenshots, puzzle guidance, and a complete Chapter 1 Demo walkthrough. The playable browser edition is available from the main attraction frame on our homepage. Official downloads, creator announcements, patch notes, and definitive release information should always be checked on StudioBigBun's itch.io page.
