Questions and answers / 05

Before the microphone goes live.

Permission, input choice, voice packs, local play, Dub Mode, consent, copyright, judging, and vocal comfort.

01What is The Choicer Voicer?+

The Choicer Voicer is a customizable microphone party game about vocal impressions in game-show formats.

02How do I play online?+

Launch the embedded player, allow microphone access, select available content, listen to a prompt, and perform into the chosen input.

03Why is microphone permission required?+

Voice is the primary gameplay input, so both browser and operating-system permission are necessary.

04Why is my microphone silent?+

Check the site permission icon, system privacy settings, selected input, other recording apps, iframe focus, and a basic stereo output setup.

05What is a voice pack?+

A voice pack is a collection of audio prompts. Begin with a short familiar set containing only files you created, licensed, or have permission to use.

06How many players can join locally?+

The published project information describes simple local multiplayer for up to four players.

07What is Dub Mode?+

Dub Mode supports voiceover performances across scenes, emphasizing timing and sustained character delivery.

08Does it have online multiplayer?+

No. The developer states that online multiplayer is not currently available.

09Are automated judges always accurate?+

No. Treat their responses as playful comparative feedback rather than an objective assessment of acting ability.

10How should we handle voice privacy?+

Explain what is recorded, let anyone decline, avoid sharing takes without permission, and delete unwanted recordings.

11How can I avoid vocal strain?+

Use conversational volume, stay hydrated, keep sessions short, and stop if speaking becomes uncomfortable.
