Questions and answers / 05

Before the next pen leaves the desk.

Controls, objective, force, computer play, mobile input, browser loading, and practical improvement.

01What is Pen Fight?+

Pen Fight is a single-player browser physics duel where you launch a red pen and try to knock a blue computer opponent off a desk.

02How do I control the red pen?+

Click or press the red pen, drag opposite the direction you want to travel, judge the guide and force, then release.

03What is the objective?+

Push the blue pen beyond the desktop boundary while keeping the red pen safely on the playable surface.

04Why does my pen move opposite the drag?+

The control behaves like a slingshot. Pulling backward stores force for movement along the opposite line.

05Is maximum power the strongest strategy?+

Not always. Excess force creates overshoot and recoil, while a moderate hit can preserve a much better next position.

06Does Pen Fight have online multiplayer?+

This browser edition presents a duel against a blue computer-controlled opponent rather than live online matchmaking.

07Can I play Pen Fight on mobile?+

Touch may work, but a mouse or trackpad on desktop gives more repeatable control over angle and pull distance.

08Why is the game frame blank?+

Wait for the external assets, click inside the iframe, refresh once, and check blockers or networks that restrict scripts, WebGL, or embedded pages.

09How can I get better at Pen Fight?+

Keep the red pen near the center, observe final positions, and change only one part of the launch after each miss.
