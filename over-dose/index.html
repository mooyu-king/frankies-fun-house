Questions and answers / 05

Before returning to the routine.

Demo status, Evren, meters, exploration, Cafe Rush, aftercare, browser loading, and player comfort.

01What is Over Dose?+

Over Dose is a psychological visual novel demo about medication, Anxiety, Awareness, room exploration, aftercare, and a complicated relationship with the doctor Evren.

02Can I play Over Dose online here?+

Yes. Press Play to load the supplied browser build without a separate installation.

03Who is Evren?+

The visible patient chart describes Evren as a 26-year-old doctor whose connection to the protagonist feels unusually familiar.

04What do Anxiety and Awareness mean?+

They provide different kinds of route feedback; Anxiety reflects pressure while Awareness reflects recognition, and neither is a complete morality score.

05Does the game include exploration?+

Yes. The screenshots show movement between rooms, clickable objects, an exit option, and an item interface.

06What is Cafe Rush?+

It is a timed service task where the player handles one customer at a time and prepares the requested drink.

07What is Aftercare?+

Aftercare is a quieter option associated with recovery and reflection after an intense scene.

08Is the route result official?+

No. The result below is an unofficial spoiler-light reflection based only on your selections.

09Can I play on mobile?+

A modern mobile browser may work, though desktop fullscreen provides clearer text and more reliable timed controls.

10Why is the iframe blank?+

Wait for remote assets, click inside the frame, refresh once, and check extensions or networks that block scripts and iframes.

11Is Over Dose suitable for children?+

Review it first; it includes medication, distress, anxiety, dependency, possible manipulation, blood marks, and a troubling doctor-patient dynamic.
