Where is the Phone Receiver?

Find the missing phone receiver in the bedroom closet, open the bedroom with the loose handle, and call Big Top Pizza in Chapter 1 Demo V2.

Demo V2 · Source checkedUpdated September 16, 20265–7 min read

Need the short route?

Quick answer

The receiver is in the bedroom closet, among boxes and hanging clothes. First take the loose bedroom door handle from the fuse-box area. Reconnect the receiver to the home telephone, then dial 244-555-7437.

Before you search the bedroom

This item belongs to the apartment chapter, after the office and elevator. It is not a prison-cell item. Follow the food and power objectives first; finding the telephone early does not skip those dependencies.

  1. Food and power

    Find the cup noodles and use the kitchen kettle. When the power fails, inspect the fuse box.

  2. Bedroom access

    Pick up the loose door handle near the fuse box. Use it on the bedroom door's missing fitting.

  3. Receiver and call

    Search the closet, return to the telephone, and complete the pizza call.

Need a nudge first?

Open one hint at a time, or jump straight to the full solution. Revealed hints are remembered on this browser.

Hint 1 · Which room?＋

The missing part is not beside the telephone. Look in the room that cannot be opened until you replace its handle.

Hint 2 · Where in that room?＋

Search the bedroom storage rather than the bed. Look down into the closet, around the small boxes and hanging clothes.

Full solution: closet → telephone → pizza

Reveal the exact steps and pizza number＋

  1. Open the bedroom

    Inspect the fuse-box area and collect the door handle. Select it from the backpack and attach it to the bedroom door, then enter.

  2. Take the receiver

    Face the bedroom closet. Scan the lower storage area among the boxes and hanging clothes. Center the receiver until its interaction prompt appears, then pick it up.

  3. Repair the telephone

    Return to the home phone. Select the receiver from the inventory and use it on the phone base. Finish any interaction animation before using the keypad.

  4. Order the pizza

    Dial 2445557437 (written as 244-555-7437). Complete the call and wait for the next objective. This phone number is fixed; it is unrelated to the prison's randomized code.

What happens next?

Follow the bath objective. When the knocking starts, leave the bathroom and answer the front door. Continue the apartment walkthrough →

Receiver or phone not responding?

The bedroom is still locked

Finding the fuse box is not the same as collecting the handle. Check your backpack, then use the handle on the door fitting.

I can see the receiver but cannot pick it up

Leave inspection mode, re-center the object, and approach from a different angle. Verify that the game has input focus. If a controller cursor is frozen, check the Virtual Mouse Speed setting or reset the game controls.

The number does not advance the objective

Confirm the receiver is attached, enter all ten digits in order, and allow the call dialogue to finish. Do not enter a prison code here. Reload only after checking the current objective and inventory; starting a new run can discard progress.
