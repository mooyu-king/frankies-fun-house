Chapter 1 Demo · Field guide

Dial Puzzles & Colored Clues

Solve the balloon, cake, fortune-teller and cotton-candy puzzles. Find the missing blue and pink dials and record all four prison-lock clues in Demo V2.

Demo V2 · Source checkedUpdated September 16, 20265–7 min read

Need the short route?

Quick answer

The lock has four colors. Red and green are already installed; find the blue dial in the cake and the pink dial on the bed after the fortune-teller interaction. The green balloon, blue fortune, pink cotton candy, and red candles give your run's numbers.

Finding a dial is not finding its number

A physical dial completes the mechanism; a clue tells you which number to set. The blue dial is hidden in the cake, but the cake's candles give the red number. This distinction prevents the most common color mix-up.

Progressive hints

Hint 1 · Treat the room as a chain＋

Birthday objects do more than decorate the room. Food can reveal an item and also become something to trade.

Hint 2 · Match the machines＋

The pump needs the balloon. The cotton-candy machine needs a rod hidden behind the poster. The fortune teller needs money from the birthday card.

Green: pop the balloon

The green dial is already on the lock. What you need is its value.

  1. Collect the loose balloon

    Search the cell and pick up the balloon; select it from your backpack.

  2. Use the hand pump

    Attach the balloon and operate the pump repeatedly until it pops.

  3. Record the reveal

    Read the revealed number and label it green. Do not confuse the number of pump actions with the number shown by the clue.

Blue: cake dial, fortune number

  1. Read red, then eat the cake

    Count the red candles first. Remove the cover and keep eating until the blue dial is exposed. Collect the dial and keep the available cake piece for Frankie.

  2. Trade the piece for a birthday card

    Give the cake piece to Frankie during her visit. Inspect the card she gives you and take the dollar.

  3. Ask the fortune teller

    Insert the dollar and activate the machine. Read or listen to its rhymed lucky number; that is blue. Completing this interaction also makes the pink dial available on the bed.

Pink: cotton-candy number, bed dial

  1. Recover the rod behind the poster

    Inspect the poster fasteners. The red pins are the weak ones. Pull them to expose the gap and retrieve the rod.

  2. Make the cotton candy

    Insert the rod into the cotton-candy machine and switch it on. Read the number formed by the candy. Write it down as pink, then turn the machine off.

  3. Collect the physical pink dial

    After finishing the fortune teller, check the bed. The candy gives a number, not the missing dial itself.

Full solution and checklist

Reveal the recommended puzzle order＋
  1. Count the red candles before disturbing the cake.
  2. Find the rod behind the poster and reveal the pink cotton-candy number.
  3. Pop the balloon and record green.
  4. Eat the cake, take the blue dial, and keep the cake piece.
  5. Trade the piece for the birthday card and take its dollar.
  6. Use the fortune teller for blue, then collect the pink dial from the bed.
  7. Install both missing parts and set the four-color prison lock.
Keep the room presentable

Do puzzle work while Frankie is away. Turn off the machine, replace the cake cover, and restore open furniture when possible before she returns.

Mark each color only after you have both its clue and the required dial. This checklist does not inspect your game save.
