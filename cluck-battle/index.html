Questions and answers / 04

Before the microphone goes live.

Browser access, safe input, cooperative setup, troubleshooting, mobile play, and video availability.

01Can I play Cluck Battle online?+

Yes. Launch the embedded browser game and allow microphone access.

02What controls the bar?+

Microphone level during the three-second round moves the contest; no specific word is required.

03Must I sound like a chicken?+

No. Clucking fits the joke, but comfortable speech or a sustained sound can work.

04Should I scream?+

No. Adjust distance and gain, use a sustainable voice, and stop if you feel strain.

05Why does the meter not move?+

Confirm browser and system permission, select the microphone, close audio apps, and reload once.

06Can friends compete locally?+

Yes. Short rounds support pass-and-play with consistent settings and attempt limits.

07Does it work on mobile?+

It may work in a current mobile browser, although microphone behavior varies.

08Are videos available?+

Not yet. Accurate videos can be added later without changing the playable page.

09Is this an official page?+

No. It is an independent portal with original setup and replay guidance.
