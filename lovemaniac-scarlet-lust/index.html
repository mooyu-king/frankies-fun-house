Questions / 05

Before the midnight attraction opens.

Age restriction, controls, verified ending, loading, mobile play, and practice.

01Is LoveManiac Scarlet Lust for adults only?+

Yes. It contains explicit sexual material and is restricted to adults aged 18 or older where such content is lawful.

02Who developed the game?+

Team Nebula and Wavis ;P are credited on the in-development release.

03What are the keyboard controls?+

Use Arrow keys to select commands and move the heart, Z to confirm or act, and X to cancel or go back. Enter does not confirm.

04How do I reach the verified ending animations?+

Complete the normal battle: keep selecting FIGHT, survive, reduce opponent HP to zero, and allow the final sequence to finish.

05Are secret MERCY, ACT, or no-hit endings confirmed?+

No separate route using those conditions is officially confirmed for version 0.1.2.

06Why does the browser game load slowly?+

Its media package is substantial. Keep one tab visible and allow the download and preparation to finish before refreshing.

07Can I play on a phone?+

Touchscreen support is listed, but performance varies. Landscape, a current browser, and free device memory are recommended.

08Why do Arrow keys or Z not respond?+

Click inside the game canvas first and confirm that focus is not in the browser toolbar or another field.

09What is a useful practice method?+

Name one difficult pattern, preserve all earlier successful movement, and change only the first correction used against that pattern.
