Privacy Policy

This policy explains how FrankiesFunHouse.org may handle information when you visit. The site does not currently provide user accounts, comments, purchases, or a working embedded game.

Technical and hosting data

Our hosting provider may process standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, device type, requested pages, timestamps, referring pages, and security events. This information is commonly required to deliver the website, maintain reliability, prevent abuse, and diagnose errors.

Browser storage

The current site does not intentionally save game progress. Basic browser features, security systems, or a future game embed may use cookies or local storage. If a browser player is added, this policy will be updated to explain its storage behavior. You can clear or restrict browser storage through your browser settings, though doing so may remove future saved preferences or progress.

Third-party links and media

The site links to the creator's itch.io page and displays locally stored promotional images. When you follow an external link, that service receives information under its own privacy policy. A future embedded game or video may also send technical data to its host; such services will be identified when added.

Analytics and advertising

We do not currently include an analytics dashboard or advertising script in this framework. If those services are introduced, this policy will be revised to identify the provider and available visitor choices.

Your choices and contact

You may use browser controls to block storage, limit referrer information, or clear site data. For privacy questions or requests related to this site, email mooyuking@gmail.com.
