Visitor information

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how FrankiesFunHouse.org and the services embedded in it may process information when you visit. The site does not provide user accounts, comments, or purchases.

Technical and hosting data

Our hosting and security providers may process standard technical information such as IP address, browser and device type, requested URL, timestamp, referring page, response status, and security events. This information is used to deliver the website, maintain reliability, prevent abuse, and diagnose errors.

Traffic analytics

The site loads a traffic measurement script from data.1back.link after the page becomes interactive. Requests are sent to https://data.1back.link/api/send with the site identifier frankiesfunhouse.org. The service may receive technical request information such as IP address, browser details, visited page, referrer, and time of access. We use aggregate traffic information to understand page use and improve the website, not to provide user accounts or sell products.

Advertising services

The site uses advertising scripts served through effectivecpmnetwork.com and highperformanceformat.com. These services may display native, banner, floating, or pop-up advertising and may process IP address, browser and device information, page URL, referrer, ad impressions, clicks, cookies, or similar identifiers for delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and ad selection. Advertisements and their destination pages are controlled by third parties, not FrankiesFunHouse.org.

Browser storage and game progress

The Discovery Tracker and embedded browser games may use local storage, IndexedDB, cookies, or similar browser-managed storage for preferences, checklist state, saves, and technical operation. Clearing site data, using private browsing, changing profiles, or moving to another device may remove that information. The embedded games are hosted on dedicated third-party or game subdomains and may manage storage separately from this website.

Guide hints and checklists

The guide pages use ffh_guide_progress_v1 in this browser's localStorage for revealed hints, solved markers, completed chapters, the last visited guide, and reading preferences. This is a personal checklist, not a copy of your game save. The Reset guide progress control on the Guides page removes only this guide entry; it does not erase embedded game saves or Discovery Tracker data. The guide progress store itself does not transmit these values to a server.

YouTube videos and external services

Gameplay videos are embedded from YouTube. Loading or playing a video can connect your browser to YouTube and Google, which may receive device, network, page, cookie, and interaction information under their own privacy terms. The site also links to itch.io and other external pages. When you follow those links, the destination service handles information according to its own policies.

Your choices and contact

You can block third-party scripts, restrict cookies or browser storage, clear stored data, and limit referrer information through browser settings or privacy extensions. Blocking embedded services may prevent games, videos, saves, analytics, or advertisements from working. For privacy questions or requests related to this site, email mooyuking@gmail.com.
